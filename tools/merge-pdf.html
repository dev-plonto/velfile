    How to merge PDF files

    1. Choose two or more PDF files.
    2. Move files up or down to set the order.
    3. Press Merge PDFs, then Download PDF.

    Common questions

    How many PDFs can I merge?

    Up to 20 files at a time.

    Will forms and bookmarks stay?

    Pages are copied as they are, but interactive parts such as form fields or bookmarks may not carry over.

    Is my file uploaded?

    No. The file is processed in your browser on your device and is not sent to a server.
