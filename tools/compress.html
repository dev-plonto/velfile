How to compress an image

  1. Choose an image (JPG, PNG or WEBP) or drop it on the box.
  2. Move the Quality slider. Lower quality gives a smaller file, and the result updates right away.
  3. Check the sizes, then press Download.

Common questions

Why doesn't my PNG get smaller?

PNG keeps every detail, so quality can't be lowered. Save as JPG or WebP for a much smaller file.

Which quality should I pick?

Around 70 to 80 percent usually looks very close to the original but is much smaller.

Is my image uploaded?

No. The image is processed in your browser on your device and is not sent to a server.
