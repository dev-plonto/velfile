Privacy

What Velfile does with your files and your data.

Your files stay on your device

Velfile's tools (Compress Image, Resize Image, Crop Image, Image Converter, JPG to PDF, Merge PDF, Split PDF, Rotate PDF, Compress PDF and PDF to JPG) run in your browser. The files you choose are read and processed on your own device. Velfile does not upload them to a server and cannot see them.

Passing a file from the home page to a tool

When you pick a file on the home page and open a tool, the file is saved temporarily in your browser's storage so the tool can open it. The tool removes it as soon as it picks it up. If you never open the tool, the copy is removed the next time the Velfile home page opens, or when you clear this site's data.

What Velfile does not do

  • No account or sign-up is needed.
  • Velfile sets no cookies of its own, and has no analytics.
  • Your files are never used for advertising. Ads do not see them.

Third-party services

Some parts of the site come from other companies, so your browser connects to them when a page loads:

  • Netlify hosts the site. Like most hosts, it may keep technical logs such as IP addresses and requested pages.
  • Google Fonts provides the fonts, loaded from Google's servers.
  • Google AdSense serves the ads (see Advertising below).
  • cdnjs provides the libraries used by the PDF tools (pdf-lib, pdf.js and JSZip). Only the libraries are loaded from there. Your files are not sent.

Advertising

Velfile shows ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. This is separate from your files, which stay on your device. You can manage personalized ads in Google Ads Settings, or learn more in how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

Changes

If Velfile adds analytics or anything else that changes this page, the page will be updated. Questions? See the Contact page.
